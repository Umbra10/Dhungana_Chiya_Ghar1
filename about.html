About Us

Dhungana Chiya Ghar is run by its owner, Ayush Dhungana, in Bhaktapur. What began as a corner stall serving students and shopkeepers is now a small tea house where the kettle is never cold.

We keep the menu short and the quality steady. Our tea is boiled with fresh milk, crushed ginger and cardamom, and served in glasses the way Bhaktapur has always taken it. Snacks come out of the kitchen warm, and the prices stay fair for students.
